About

Hi, I'm Dodo — a physio who makes house calls.

I trained at Glasgow Caledonian University and spent eight years at Singapore General Hospital before starting followme physiotherapy. I've also served as a therapist with the Singapore Cancer Society, and I'm passionate about empowering people through movement — at every age and every stage.

"Follow me" isn't a slogan. It's how I practise. I meet you where you are — in your living room, by your bed, on the corridor outside your HDB — and we walk the recovery one step at a time.

Services

Care that comes to you

Every visit is one-to-one, unhurried, and built around what your body — and your life — actually needs this week.

  • Geriatric rehabilitation

    Strength, balance, and confidence for ageing bodies — so your loved ones keep doing what they love.

  • Cancer rehabilitation

    Active recovery during and after treatment — regain strength, manage fatigue, and rebuild the body you live in.

  • Schroth for scoliosis

    A specialised, exercise-based method for scoliosis that teaches your spine a better, kinder posture.

  • Falls prevention & balance

    Assess the real cause of unsteadiness and train the systems — strength, vision, confidence — that keep you upright.

  • Post-surgical & orthopaedic

    Knee replacements, shoulders, spines — structured protocols to get you moving without fear of setbacks.

  • Parkinson's & neurological

    Large-amplitude, dignity-first programmes for Parkinson's, stroke, and neurodegenerative conditions.

  • Postpartum & pelvic health

    Return-to-strength for new mothers — core, pelvic floor, and the practical mechanics of carrying a baby.

  • Swelling & lymphoedema

    Decongestive techniques and home routines after breast cancer treatment or chronic swelling.

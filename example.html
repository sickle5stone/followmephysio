About Dodo

I am Dodo Wee, a physiotherapist who believes that true healing happens where we feel most at home. After years in hospital wards, I realized that clinical excellence is only half the journey—the other half is the environment you return to.

My approach is deeply personal and domestic. We don't just "exercise"; we practice the movements that matter to you. Whether it's reaching for the favorite tea tin or walking down to the market, we build strength into the fabric of your day.
